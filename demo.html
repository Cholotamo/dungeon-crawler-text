Modular Retro Terminal - Isolation Test Page

This page uses a completely blank, un-themed backdrop with standard browser fonts to test style isolation.

  • Draggable: Click and drag the top title bar (┌─[ cholo@portfolio:~ ]─┐) to move the terminal anywhere.
  • Interactive: Click inside the terminal to focus. Type simulated commands (e.g. help, about, skills, clear).
  • Blinking Cursor: Notice the retro blinking cursor next to cholo> |.
  • Retro Controls: Click [_] to minimize to a titlebar strip or [X] to reset.
  • Zero Leakage: All terminal styles, fonts, and CRT scanlines are locked inside its Shadow DOM.
