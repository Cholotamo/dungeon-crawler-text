Modular Retro Terminal - Isolation Test Page

This page uses a completely blank, un-themed backdrop to verify style isolation.

  • Scroll Past It: The terminal is positioned in the document flow (position: absolute). Scroll down to see it left behind.
  • Draggable Anywhere: Drag the top bar (dungeon-crawler-text) to place it anywhere on the page—even after scrolling down.
  • Typewriter Lore Prose: Types out each paragraph with its own loremaster > prompt character by character. (Skipping is disabled to let the atmosphere unfold).
  • Sequential Map Mutation: Spawns immediately on Enter in standby; paragraph 0 starts the 32x32 radar dot scan, and after each subsequent paragraph types out, the prose pauses while the map mutates the relevant regional biomes character-by-character, culminating in the complete terrain map!
  • Tethered Window Lifecycle: Both terminals are cross-tethered by the [X] button—closing either terminal closes both!
  • Self-Contained: All story text is embedded directly into retro-terminal.js—zero external fetch requests or dependencies needed.
  • Square Form Factor: Styled in a clean 1:1 aspect ratio matching the viewer's grid styling (responsive 310px on mobile).
  • Active Window Focus: Clicking or dragging either window elevates it to the top layer.
  • Minimize / Close: Click [_] to collapse it, or [X] to kill it completely from the DOM.
  • Test Spawner: Click the floating [+] Spawn Terminal button at the bottom-right to bring it back (guaranteed single instance).
