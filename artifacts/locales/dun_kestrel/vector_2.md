# Locale Evolution Vector: Dun Kestrel

## Global Developments
- **The Ward-Fosse (Waterworks):** Facing the suffocating death-miasma and prowling wights of Mor-Ghul, river-engineers from Aethelford excavated a massive defensive canal from across to, diverting the swift, mountain-fed torrents of the Silver Channel into an impassable running-water moat that arrests the northern advance of the Blighted Mire.
- **Eaveshold:** Anchored securely behind the torrent of the Ward-Fosse, the beleaguered border outpost was rebuilt in cyclopean granite and ironwood, raising high ballista towers, deep moats, and consecrated beacon-fires to stand as the sovereign marcher citadel of the Greenwood.
- **The Marcher Garths (Domain Expansion):** With their southern flank shielded by running water, the garrisons of Eaveshold cleared the dense hemlock timber and into productive, leat-irrigated barley and rye fields to ensure the fortress could endure protracted sieges without reliance on river barges.
- **Ironcrest:** Founded in the northern foothills of the Undulating Downs beneath the precipices of the Northern Ribs by the High Thane of Dun Kestrel, serving as a heavily fortified redoubt to mine exposed veins of magnetite iron and quarry dark slate for the highland host.
- **The Iron-Trace:** A graded mountain causeway paved with crushed quartzite and basalt ballast, engineered to haul heavy cartloads of smelted iron blooms and building stone south from Ironcrest into the armories of Dun Kestrel.
- **The Northern Ribs (Region Lore Mutation):** The desolate granite spurs and alpine screes now echo with the clang of highland pickaxes and the smoke of charcoal bloomery kilns as Dun Kestrel drives deep mines into the ancient roots of the peaks.
- **The Greenwood Vault (Region Lore Mutation):** The choking terror that gripped the eastern woodland has been broken by the rushing waters of the Ward-Fosse, allowing mortal axes and stone towers to permanently tame the northern margins of the ancient forest.

## Locale (Dun Kestrel) Developments

### Road New Development
- **The Iron-Trace (NORTH):** New road established leading toward Ironcrest (Outpost). A steep, graded wagon-track of crushed limestone and basalt ballast linking the high citadel of Dun Kestrel with the northern iron mines of Ironcrest.

### Neighbouring Destination New Development
- **Ironcrest (NORTH via The Iron-Trace):** Leads toward Ironcrest (Outpost). A fortified highland mining redoubt established in the shadow of the Northern Ribs, founded by the High Thane of Dun Kestrel to mine rich veins of magnetite iron and quarry dark slate for the highland citadel.
