# Locale Evolution Vector: Eaveshold

## Global Developments
- **Ironcrest:** Surging from a rudimentary mining camp into an unyielding mountain fortress-citadel, the clan smiths and engineers of Dun Kestrel drove colossal vertical adits into the Northern Ribs, raising roaring charcoal blast furnaces and slate-faced curtain walls to forge sovereign hegemony over the northern passes and supply heavy magnetite steel to the realm.
- **Confluence Watch:** An armada of armed river-cogs and heavy timber-keels sailed south from Aethelford to the great confluence of the Silver Channel and the Jade River, clearing the dense hemlocks and into a fortified river-bastion, naval quay, and iron-chained water-gate designed to bottle up necrotic seepage from Mor-Ghul and secure waterborne passage toward the southern sea.
- **The Northern Ribs (Region Lore Mutation):** The desolate alpine screes and towering granite crags now thunder day and night with the deep tremors of heavy excavation, blast furnaces, and cascading slag-sluices, as the newly elevated citadel of Ironcrest delves labyrinthine adits into the ancient mountain roots to harvest vast deposits of black magnetite iron and dark roofing slate.
- **The Undulating Downs (Region Lore Mutation):** Anchored between the twin sovereign citadels of Dun Kestrel and Ironcrest, the high rolling chalklands have transformed into a fortified highland realm of martial discipline, deep limestone quarries, terraced barley slopes, and rumbling iron-wains traversing the paved trace.
- **The Silent Meeting (Region Lore Mutation):** The primeval solitude of the great sister-confluence has yielded to the ring of iron and the splash of heavy oars; armed river-galleys and stone water-gates from Confluence Watch now patrol the swirling silver and jade eddies, maintaining vigilant quarantine against the poisoned runoff and wandering wights of the northern marshes.
- **The Greenwood Vault (Region Lore Mutation):** The ancient hemlock canopy remains dense and primeval, but its shadowed solitude is now bookended by mortal steel—anchored in the northeast by the battlements of Eaveshold and in the deep south by the water-gates of Confluence Watch, whose lumbermen fell giant river-trunks to supply the shipwrights of the lower basin.
