## The Years of High Water — Years 79–126

### 1. Year 79 — The Black Flood and the Founding of Highbank

In the spring of Year 79, snowmelt descended violently through the **River Vael** [X: 16, Y: 19]. The piers of **Vaelbridge** [X: 16, Y: 19] trapped uprooted trees and narrowed the torrent until the western bank failed. Water entered **Vaelmark** [X: 15, Y: 19], drowning its granaries and carrying bodies south among the reeds.

The flood permanently claimed the cultivated ground at [X: 12, Y: 24] and [X: 13, Y: 24], turning those fields of the **Vaelplain** into new marshland contiguous with the **Serefen** [X: 14, Y: 24]. Survivors thereafter called this drowned tract the **Widows’ Mere**.

Those unwilling to rebuild beside the river climbed northwestern ground and founded **Highbank** [X: 14, Y: 16], an outpost upon the **Barrow Hills** [X: 14, Y: 16]. Its height placed it beyond ordinary floods, while nearby slopes offered grazing and exposed stone for foundations.

The **Highbank Spur** was laid from the **Oris–Vael Track** [X: 15, Y: 18] through [X: 14, Y: 17] to **Highbank** [X: 14, Y: 16]. In the aftermath, the channel beside **Vaelbridge** [X: 16, Y: 19] was widened, but its old stone piers remained—a monument to pride and necessity alike.

### 2. Years 103–126 — The Eastern Iron Road

When iron tools became scarce, prospectors crossed **Vaelbridge** [X: 16, Y: 19] and followed the northern margin of **Gravenwood** [X: 18, Y: 18]. In Year 103 they discovered red ironstone and copper-bearing slate where the **High Veld** [X: 26, Y: 13] meets the **Ashen Teeth** [X: 25, Y: 13].

They founded **Cinderwatch** [X: 26, Y: 13] beside the upper **River Nareth** [X: 27, Y: 13]. The outpost guarded shallow mines, smelting hearths, and the only dependable watering place beneath those barren slopes.

By Year 126, the **Cinder Road** connected **Vaelbridge** [X: 16, Y: 19] to **Cinderwatch** [X: 26, Y: 13], following:

`(17,19) → (18,18) → (19,18) → (20,17) → (21,16) → (22,15) → (23,14) → (24,13) → (25,13)`

Road crews felled a diagonal wound through **Gravenwood** [X: 17, Y: 19] and its northern trees. Charcoal burners widened the damage, feeding timber into the hungry furnaces of **Cinderwatch** [X: 26, Y: 13].

The cutting drove wolves and darker things inward. Beneath the dense trees of **Gravenwood Deep Groves** [X: 21, Y: 19], displaced beasts gathered around a vast antlered predator. Their lair became known as **The Hollow Crown** [X: 21, Y: 19], and caravans soon began vanishing within sight of their own campfires.
