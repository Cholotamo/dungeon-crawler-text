## The Gateward Ascendancy — Years 176–219

### 1. Years 176–188 — Orisward Becomes a City

The reopening of the **Cinder Road** [X: 20, Y: 17] brought iron eastward and grain westward. At **Orisward** [X: 06, Y: 18], iron nails and saws transformed crude boatbuilding into a shipwright’s trade. In Year 176, the outpost was walled and proclaimed a city.

Its prosperity consumed the southern margin of **Elderweald** [X: 06, Y: 19]. The forest tiles at [X: 05, Y: 19], [X: 06, Y: 19], [X: 05, Y: 20], and [X: 06, Y: 20] were felled for hulls, wharves, charcoal, and palisades. Open timber yards replaced the canopy, and rain began washing bare soil into **Bay of Oris** [X: 06, Y: 17].

By Year 188, silt had shallowed the old landing places. Labourers drove wooden quays farther into **Bay of Oris** [X: 06, Y: 17], while storms increasingly flooded the stripped ground south of **Orisward** [X: 06, Y: 18]. Wealth had made the city stronger, but its harbour had begun to choke upon the remains of the forest that built it.

### 2. Years 194–219 — The Opening of Vaelgate

Seeking direct access to the northern slopes, surveyors departed **Highbank** [X: 14, Y: 16] in Year 194. They followed the western bank of the **River Vael** [X: 16, Y: 14], climbing through the **Barrow Hills** [X: 15, Y: 14] toward **Vaelgate Pass** [X: 16, Y: 05].

The resulting **Vaelgate Road** ran through:

`(15,15) → (15,14) → (15,13) → (15,12) → (15,11) → (15,10) → (15,09) → (15,08) → (15,07) → (15,06) → (16,05)`

At [X: 15, Y: 06], hundreds of workers cut a shelf through the **Ironspine Range** [X: 15, Y: 06]. Spoil was cast downslope into nearby streams, muddying the upper **River Vael** [X: 16, Y: 06] each spring. The road remained narrow enough that two loaded wagons could not pass abreast.

In Year 207, masons founded **Stonewake** [X: 16, Y: 05] within **Vaelgate Pass** [X: 16, Y: 05]. The outpost guarded the road, sheltered winter caravans, and taxed iron, salt, and furs moving through the mountains.

Then, in Year 219, an earthquake sealed the northern mouth of **Vaelgate Pass** [X: 16, Y: 05]. **Stonewake** [X: 16, Y: 05] survived, but the collapse exposed a seam of black iron within its southern wall. Mining began immediately. Hammering continued beneath the fortress day and night, and old soldiers swore that something deeper answered every blow with one of its own.
