## The War Beneath the Boughs — Years 131–168

### 1. Years 131–143 — The Antlered Host

The disappearances along the **Cinder Road** [X: 19, Y: 18] worsened after the winter of Year 131. The creatures sheltering within **The Hollow Crown** [X: 21, Y: 19] ceased hunting as scattered beasts and emerged as an organized host: wolves driven ahead as hounds, pale forest-men bearing bone spears, and the great antlered predator called the Horned Father.

In Year 136, the host destroyed an ore caravan at [X: 20, Y: 17]. Traffic ceased beyond that point, isolating **Cinderwatch** [X: 26, Y: 13] from food and replacement tools. Miners abandoned the deepest workings, and many fled west toward **Vaelmark** [X: 15, Y: 19] across **Vaelbridge** [X: 16, Y: 19].

During the Bitter Winter of Year 143, starving beasts followed the refugees. They reached the eastern approaches of **Vaelmark** [X: 15, Y: 19], but could not force **Vaelbridge** [X: 16, Y: 19]. The bridge became a barricade of wagons, sharpened stakes, and corpses frozen together by river ice.

### 2. Years 149–168 — Ashgate and the Blackwood Burning

In Year 149, **Vaelmark** [X: 15, Y: 19], **Highbank** [X: 14, Y: 16], and **Orisward** [X: 06, Y: 18] mustered their first common war-band. Rather than assault **The Hollow Crown** [X: 21, Y: 19] beneath its dense canopy, they fortified the narrow approach beside the **Cinder Road** [X: 19, Y: 18].

There they founded **Ashgate** [X: 19, Y: 17], a timber-and-earth outpost positioned to protect caravans and prevent the antlered host from moving west. The woodland at [X: 19, Y: 16], [X: 18, Y: 17], and [X: 19, Y: 17] was cleared for sightlines. A short approach named the **Ashgate Spur** joined the fort to the **Cinder Road** through [X: 19, Y: 18].

The war-band then burned the forest around the enemy lair. Pitch trenches turned the dense groves at [X: 20, Y: 19], [X: 21, Y: 20], and [X: 20, Y: 21] into lifeless black earth. Smoke hung over **Gravenwood** [X: 20, Y: 20] for an entire summer, killing game and poisoning shallow wells.

The Horned Father was slain outside **Ashgate** [X: 19, Y: 17] in Year 168, but the victors never entered the deepest chamber of **The Hollow Crown** [X: 21, Y: 19]. Beneath its roots they heard many smaller antlers scraping against stone. The dungeon endured, surrounded by the new wasteland called the **Crownscar**.

Ore traffic resumed along the **Cinder Road** [X: 20, Y: 17], though every caravan thereafter travelled under armed escort to **Cinderwatch** [X: 26, Y: 13].
