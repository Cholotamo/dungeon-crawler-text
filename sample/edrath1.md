## The First Reckoning — Year 1

The known world lies upon a broad temperate landmass called **Edrath**, bounded by cold seas and broken coasts. Its western half is wet, wooded, and storm-beaten; its eastern reaches are drier, higher, and scarred by old stone. Winters grow severe toward the north, while the southern shores remain mild but frequently lashed by rain.

### The Encircling Waters

To the west spreads the **Mournful Ocean**, a grey and violent expanse whose tides have carved Edrath into cliffs, narrow beaches, and drowned coves. Near the middle of the western coast, the sea enters deeply through the **Bay of Oris**, a broad natural harbor sheltered by two long, rocky headlands.

The northern coast faces the **Pale Sea**. Drift ice gathers there during the hardest winters, though the water remains navigable in summer. The eastern shoreline borders the **Glasswater**, a comparatively calm sea lined with shingle beaches and chalk bluffs.

In the south, the land curves around the warm, rain-darkened **Gulf of Sere**. Salt marshes and tidal flats choke its innermost reaches, while the outer coast is divided by wooded peninsulas.

### The Mountain Chains

Across northern Edrath stands the **Ironspine Range**, an almost unbroken wall of black granite running from the **Mournful Ocean** toward the **Glasswater**. Snow lingers upon its highest ridges throughout the year. Only three low passes breach the chain, and all are narrow enough to be closed by landslides or winter storms.

From the central **Ironspine Range**, a younger chain called the **Ashen Teeth** descends southeastward. These mountains are lower but far more jagged, with exposed slate faces, deep ravines, and barren high valleys. The ground beneath them is rich in iron, copper, and coal, though frequent tremors make the slopes treacherous.

Along the western coast, the shorter **Stormward Heights** rise abruptly behind the sea cliffs. They trap rain from the **Mournful Ocean**, feeding countless streams and leaving the lands east of them noticeably drier.

### The Rivers and Lowlands

The greatest river is the **River Vael**, born from snowmelt in the central **Ironspine Range**. It flows south through a long, fertile basin before dividing into the reed-choked **Vael Delta** at the **Gulf of Sere**. Seasonal floods lay down rich black soil across its banks, but in wet years the same floods drown entire stretches of lowland.

West of the **River Vael**, the swift **River Or** descends from the **Stormward Heights** and empties into the **Bay of Oris**. Its upper course runs through steep gorges; farther west, it broadens into a navigable waterway surrounded by damp meadows.

The **River Nareth** rises beneath the eastern **Ironspine Range** and bends southeast toward the **Glasswater**. It passes through chalk hills and open grasslands, cutting a broad valley that forms the easiest natural passage between the interior and the eastern coast.

Farther south, the sluggish **Blackmere River** drains the ancient forests into the inner **Gulf of Sere**. Fallen trees, peat, and dark tannins stain its water almost black. Its lower course dissolves into marsh channels collectively known as the **Serefen**.

### The Ancient Forests

Northwest Edrath is dominated by **Elderweald**, a vast rain-soaked forest rooted between the **Stormward Heights** and the **River Or**. Its interior is thick with oak, yew, pine, and immense moss-covered trees older than any surviving reckoning. Little sunlight reaches its deepest floor.

South of the **Ashen Teeth** lies **Gravenwood**, a darker and drier forest of beech, hornbeam, and black fir. Stone ridges rise beneath its roots, forming caves and sudden sinkholes. At its southern edge, the woodland gives way to the marshes of the **Serefen**.

In eastern Edrath, the scattered but ancient **Whitebark Forest** follows the upper **River Nareth**. Its pale-barked trees grow upon chalk soil and low ridges, leaving wide grassy clearings between dense groves.

### The Hills and Open Country

Between **Elderweald** and the **River Vael** roll the **Barrow Hills**, a broad country of heather, exposed stone, and wind-rounded summits. Natural caves riddle the deeper slopes, while solitary earthen mounds crown many ridgelines.

East of the **River Vael**, the land opens into the **High Veld**, an immense sweep of temperate grassland broken by shallow valleys and granite outcrops. Rainfall is lighter there because of the **Stormward Heights** and the **Ashen Teeth**, yet numerous springs make the western portion fertile.

South of the **Barrow Hills**, the ground descends into the **Vaelplain**, the largest continuous stretch of arable land in Edrath. Beyond it, nearer the **Gulf of Sere**, firm soil gives way to peat bogs, salt marshes, and the drowned reed beds of the **Serefen**.

Thus Edrath begins: mountains sealing the north, forests holding the west and south, open plains stretching eastward, and three great river valleys drawing every future road toward the sea.
